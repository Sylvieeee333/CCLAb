Abyssal Pulse

Scientific Name:

Abyssal Pulse

Discovered by / at:

Sylvie Wu discovered some juvenile experiment subjects and sent them to the deep ocean for observation.

Family:

It is created as an experiment so its parent is Sylvie Wu, and it has some siblings who have similar backgrounds. They live as a small group in the habitat.

Habitat:

Deep ocean where there is no light.

Appearance:

It is a single-celled organism with a large eye and floating organelles in its body, and colorful halos flowing around its body. The main motion of the creature will be a continuous breathing cycle. Its body will slowly expand and contract, creating a soft and organic rhythm. Inside its round body, colorful organelles will float, rotate, and change their positions as the creature breathes.

Key Characteristics:

  • A large eye in the middle of its body.
  • Body movement within breathing.
  • Colorful halos flowing around.
  • Multiple organelles rotating and changing regularly.

What we know so far about Abyssal Pulse? It is a single-celled deep-sea organism, a juvenile experiment subject discovered by Sylvie Wu (a deep-sea researcher) to detect deep-sea activities for the scientists.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Its eye will follow the mouse movement, as if it is observing us. With a mouse click, BOB will get frightened and its eye will turn into a vertical pupil. .
